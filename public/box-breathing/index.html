The Science & Practice of Box Breathing

Widely practiced by tactical operators, emergency clinicians, and somatic therapists, Box Breathing is a four-cornered respiratory cycle proven to regulate cardiac pacing and reduce acute stress.

The Four Equal Corners

Inhale deeply for 4 seconds, retain air gently for 4 seconds, exhale smoothly for 4 seconds, and hold the lungs empty for 4 seconds.

Cortisol & Blood Pressure Reduction

Studies indicate that rhythmic 4-4-4 breathing optimizes carbon dioxide tolerance, expands arterial elasticity, and stabilizes heart rate variability.
